    Class CircularQueue<ItemT>

    Fixed capacity FIFO queue backed by a ring buffer. Items are added at the rear and removed from the front in O(1).

    Every traversal (forEach, filter, query, iteration, getIndex) runs from the front to the rear, and position 0 is the front. Positions are logical: they never depend on where an item sits in the ring buffer.

    The ring buffer holds maxSize slots, allocated once at construction and reused for the queue's lifetime: push, pop, insertFront, peek, getIndex, forEach, clearElements, and reset never allocate. forEach is the non-allocating way to walk the queue; for...of allocates one iterator per loop.

    Byte encoding is provided by the ByteCircularQueue subclass, which requires an ItemCodec at construction.
